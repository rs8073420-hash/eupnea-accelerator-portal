Platform Categorisation & Channel Architecture

Programme & Investment Team Routing Framework

Every evaluated directory is routed into dedicated specialist channels so programme managers focus on verified intake portals, SmartMatch networks, UK rankings, and capital syndicates without directory clutter.

1. SmartMatch & Dealflow Licensing PRIMARY

Two-sided platforms matching accelerators with early-stage founders across both free profiles and paid dealflow licences (IncubatorList, Round Funded, OpenVC, Vestbee).

2. UK Accelerator Review & Rankings UK SCOPE

Strictly UK-focused accelerator directories and analyst lists (SeedLegals, Tracxn UK, PartnershipsBuddy, Beauhurst, Sifted UK, UKTN) excluding peer accelerator blogs.

3. LinkedIn Accelerator Rankers OUTREACH

VCs, managing directors, and ecosystem analysts publishing UK/European accelerator roundups, paired with verified LinkedIn URLs and bespoke pitch drafts.

4. Direct Startup Intake Hubs INTAKE

Multi-programme registration marketplaces (F6S, Gust, YouNoodle, VC4A) where Eupnea hosts a public programme page and collects founder applications directly.

5. Investor Match & Capital Networks SYNDICATES

Angel networks, SEIS funds, and co-investment hubs (UKBAA, OION, Angel CoFund, SFC Capital) supporting cohort fundraising.

6. Ecosystem Visibility & Regional Chambers DISCOVERY

Venture registries (Crunchbase, Dealroom, StartupBlink) and regional UK Chambers of Commerce retained for partnership outreach and SEO authority.

All 7 categories routed via verified qualification rules.
